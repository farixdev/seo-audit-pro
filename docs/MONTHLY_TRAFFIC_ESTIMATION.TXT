======================================================================
MONTHLY ORGANIC TRAFFIC & TRENDS — ONE-LINER REFERENCE
======================================================================

1. WHAT THIS METRIC REPRESENTS (ONE-LINER)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Monthly Organic Traffic:
  A statistical algorithmic projection of estimated monthly search visitors based on Domain Authority (DA) and site size.

* Notice on Accuracy:
  This data is an ESTIMATION model, NOT direct Google Analytics or Search Console numbers. Actual visitor counts may vary.

* Power-Law Traffic Curve:
  Computed as DA^2.3 base multiplier with seasonal fluctuation heuristics and crawl footprint weights.

* Ranking Keywords Estimate:
  Statistical projection of organic search terms ranking in the top 100 positions based on overall authority.

* Geographic Distribution:
  Projected regional market breakdown based on the top-level domain (TLD) and international targeting signals.


2. CALCULATION PARAMETERS (AVAILABLE ON HOVER)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Domain Authority (DA) Score
* Power-Law Mathematical Curve (DA^2.3)
* Crawled Pages Footprint Volume
* TLD Regional Market Bias (.com, .ca, .co.uk, etc.)
* Seasonal 12-Month Sine Curve Multipliers
